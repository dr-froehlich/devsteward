## Context

`process.develop: split` promises a phase the engine has never had. Its sole reader in the
package is `devsteward/profiles/req/source.py:85-88`, where it sets `Step.attended = True`
with the reason *"declared a split develop — needs an attended design review"*. That is the
whole of it: unattended `steward run`/`advance` parks the step (`executor.py:566`). There is
no second session, no design-review stop, no plan sign-off, no phase boundary. `/advance`
mentions `split` **zero** times against nine mentions of `concept`, so a REQ declaring it is
worked identically to a fused one.

`concept: true` sets the *same* `attended` flag from the `elif` branch immediately below,
and adds a real land gate (`ConceptArtifactGate`). So **split is a strict subset of
concept** — it contributes nothing concept does not already contribute. Two further
consequences of the `if`/`elif`: a REQ declaring both is parked with the design-review
reason and is **never told a concept phase exists**; and plan-first, the discipline `split`
is popularly associated with, is already universal — `PlanArtifactGate` refuses the land for
*every* REQ when no file in the plans dir names its id.

The declaration therefore has **no reader**. That is the same defect shape REQ-083 retired
in the roadmap artifact, and the docs have drifted exactly as a reader-less field always
does — into three mutually incompatible definitions shipped simultaneously:

| Surface | Claimed meaning of `split` |
|---|---|
| `req.md` template, handbook `_01-format.qmd:31`, `req.schema.json` | "an attended design review before build, for genuinely risky REQs" |
| **`STEWARD.md:94`**, handbook `_00-method.qmd:61` | "a **post-freeze develop phase** authors the target ACs" |
| intake skill §2c l.232 | "an attended design review before build" |
| intake skill l.176 | "`develop: split` is its natural shape" for a live-proof-in-develop obligation |

The shipped manual's meaning is coherent only *in combination with* concept — it is defined
as what happens after the concept deliverable freezes — while the schema and handbook sell
split as a standalone risk knob. The manual is the only interface a consumer agent has
(REQ-094), so it is the manual that has been teaching the redundant pairing. The corpus
shows the teaching landed: across seven repos **19 REQs** carry an uncommented
`develop: split` and **8 of them (42%) also set `concept: true`**, evenly spread rather than
clustered — a documentation-generated pattern, not one author's habit. Neither documented
meaning is implemented by any code.

Removing the value alone would leave `fused` as the sole legal value of a key whose default
is `fused` — a field that can never again carry information. So the whole key goes. What it
cannot do is invalidate history: `steward lint` schema-validates **every** REQ including
`done` ones, and reads the **packaged** schema, so the fleet's **125** REQs carrying an
uncommented `develop:` (19 split + 106 fused, 18 of the 19 terminal) would red in seven
repos at once through the shared pipx engine, with no opt-in. Unlike REQ-083's config seam —
where `data.get(...)` gave tolerance for free — the `process` block is
`additionalProperties: false`, so here tolerance must be **declared on purpose**.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Subtract the whole `develop:` key, not just the `split` value.** It disappears from the engine, the req template, the schema's live documentation, the handbook, `STEWARD.md`, and the intake interview. `process:` reduces to `concept:` + `lab:` (+ `fixtures:`). | With `split` gone, `fused` restates the default and nothing else. Keeping it would preserve the "declaration with no reader" smell one size smaller and still cost intake a question. Owner's ruling, 2026-09-03. |
| 2 | **The schema stays tolerant: `develop` remains a declared, accepted, engine-ignored property.** Its description is rewritten to say it is a retired legacy key that the engine does not read. No lint warning, no deprecation channel, no migration verb, no sweep of consumer repos. | `additionalProperties: false` means the property must stay *declared* or 125 REQs across 7 repos red instantly through the shared engine. 18 of the 19 `split` REQs are terminal history; rewriting a done REQ's frontmatter would falsify the spec record it exists to preserve. The field was already inert — this stops pretending otherwise rather than erasing the evidence. Owner's ruling, 2026-09-03. |
| 3 | **No consumer sweep, and no consumer is asked to act.** DevSteward edits its own repo only. The one non-terminal declarer (MobileSteward `REQ-002`, `draft`, which sets both flags) keeps working unchanged: under decision 2 it stays schema-valid, and since it also declares `concept: true` it keeps the attendance it always effectively had. | The blast radius is the whole point of decision 2. | 4 | **Attendance is `concept:`-only.** After the `split` branch is deleted, `Step.attended` is set by `process.concept` alone, so the park reason is always the concept one — the message a both-flags REQ could never previously reach. | Collapses two flags with one meaning into the one that also carries a gate, and removes the `if`/`elif` shadowing outright rather than reordering it. |

## Requirement

### 1. Engine

- `devsteward/profiles/req/source.py` contains no reader of `process["develop"]`. The
  `split` branch is deleted and `Step.attended` / `attended_reason` are set by
  `process.concept` alone.
- `devsteward/profiles/req/reqfile.py`: `PROCESS_DEFAULTS` no longer carries
  `"develop": "fused"`, and its docstring stops naming a develop mode. A legacy REQ's raw
  `develop:` value merges through untouched and is read by nothing.
- `devsteward/core/model.py`: the `Step.attended` comment names the concept phase only.

### 2. Schema (tolerance, both copies)

- `process.develop` remains a **declared** property in `devsteward/schema/req.schema.json`
  and in the stamped copy, accepting its historical values, with a description stating it is
  a retired key the engine does not read.
- A REQ carrying `develop: split` or `develop: fused` lints **green**, and
  `ReqFile.process` exposes no `develop` key of its own.

### 3. Shipped surfaces

- The req templates (`docs/requirements/_templates/req.md` and the stamped
  `devsteward/templates/docs/requirements/_templates/req.md`) carry no `develop:` line in
  the commented `process:` example.
- `devsteward/templates/STEWARD.md` (the repo-root `STEWARD.md` is a symlink to it — one
  file) names no `develop:`/`split` declaration at lines 94, 141 and 247; the attended-park
  and D/H rows name the concept phase as the attended need.
- `devsteward/templates/CLAUDE.md.tmpl`: the concept bullet states that the deliverable is
  either `<concepts_dir>/REQ-NNN.md` **or** a non-empty `<concepts_dir>/REQ-NNN/` bundle
  directory, referenced from `concept_refs:`, using the configured `concepts_dir` rather
  than a hardcoded `docs/concepts/`; the flat-path-only claim is gone.
- The handbook (`_00-method.qmd:61`, `_01-format.qmd:22,31`, `_02-engine.qmd:91`,
  `_03-workflow.qmd:26,261`) names no develop mode; `process:` is described as `concept:` +
  `lab:`.
- The intake skill (`.claude/skills/intake/SKILL.md` and its stamped template are **one
  inode** — edit once) drops `develop:` from the §2c declarations, so §2c asks two
  questions; l.176's live-proof-in-develop guidance names the obligation without naming a
  flag; and §2c's phase-model fork is restated per decision 5.
- `/advance` needs no change: it already carries no `split` handling, and its concept
  section is correct.

### 4. Doctrine restatement (REQ-071)

Intake §2c, `STEWARD.md` and handbook `_00-method.qmd` state the phase-model placement as:
when `concept: true` is set because the target ACs depend on the concept deliverable, the
post-freeze work is either authored by **the concept session itself once the deliverable is
frozen**, or homed in a **named downstream REQ** — and the upstream REQ must never carry the
downstream REQ's acceptance bar.

## Notes

- **Process declarations:** none — defaults apply (no concept phase, no lab). The acceptance
  criteria are honestly writable at intake (every claim is a scan or a step-derivation
  assertion), so no concept phase is warranted; and with no `artifact`/`manual` check there
  is no System-Test phase. The REQ dogfoods its own subtraction by declaring no `process:`
  block at all.
- **Corpus at intake (2026-09-03), the basis for decision 2:** 19 REQs across 7 repos carry
  an uncommented `develop: split` — devsteward REQ-063; flowsteward 024, 026, 036, 046, 055,
  092, 135, 136, 151; DriveSteward 013, 019; docsteward 005, 006; hausverwaltung 002, 004;
  recipes 002, 005; MobileSteward 002. 18 are `done`; MobileSteward REQ-002 is the only
  `draft`. 8 also declare `concept: true`. A further 106 REQs declare `develop: fused`.
- **Out of scope — homed, not deferred (decision 8):** the packaged↔stamped schema drift
  (`process.fixtures` missing and the pre-REQ-048 `supersedes` shape in
  `devsteward/templates/docs/requirements/schema/req.schema.json`) belongs to **REQ-096**.
- **Out of scope:** any replacement for `split`. Options (b) an engine-enforced attended plan
  sign-off and (c) a real post-freeze second develop phase were both considered and rejected
  at intake — the attended session already does by being attended what a sign-off step would
  bookkeep, which is the machinery REQ-047 taught this project to stop adding.
- **Localization:** engine, schema and doc text is technical English; no user-facing
  localized strings.
- **Surfaces (expected):** `devsteward/profiles/req/source.py`,
