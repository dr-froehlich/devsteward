## Context

Unattended runs must never guess at a real fork. When the engine shells out headless it
sets `DEVSTEWARD_UNATTENDED=1`; skills then write a decision request to the ledger and
stop. The engine records it, advances to the next independent step, and resumes once a
human answers. The interview stays sacred.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|----------|-----------|
| 1 | `DEVSTEWARD_UNATTENDED=1` env signals "park, don't ask" | One clear contract between engine and skill |
| 2 | Parked step → BLOCKED; answering → PENDING | The loop skips it, then resumes it cleanly |
| 3 | Decisions live in `state.yaml`, logged in `events.jsonl` | Git-friendly, auditable |

## Requirement

The ledger parks and answers decisions. The executor detects a parked fork (a new open
decision for the step, or the `[[DEVSTEWARD_PARK]]` sentinel), leaves the step blocked,
and continues. `steward decision answer` unblocks it.
