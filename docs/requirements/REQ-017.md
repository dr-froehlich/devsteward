## Context

This preserves the original REQ-010 intent that the memzy work superseded in practice but
not in need. **ExamEngineer** still holds its requirements in the older **prose** format
(`### REQ-NNN:` headers with `- **Status:**` lines) — there is no YAML frontmatter to
normalize, so memzy's dialect normalizer ([[REQ-010]]) does not apply directly. A prose
front-end must first *parse* the headers into frontmatter, then hand off to REQ-010's
normalization core.

Held as a **draft** on purpose: ExamEngineer is mid-flight in a skill-driven "upscale"
evolution, and migrating its corpus before that settles would convert a moving target.
Promote to `open` once the upscale concludes and ExamEngineer is ready to come under the
engine.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Defer until ExamEngineer's upscale concludes | Don't convert a corpus that is still being reshaped |
| 2 | Build a **prose front-end** on top of REQ-010's core, not a parallel converter | The normalization, faithful-verdict, and lint rules are identical once frontmatter exists; only the parse step differs (a converter per project, shared core — REQ-010 Decision 6) |
| 3 | Inherit REQ-010's archivist stance | `[x]`/"met" → `passed`; manual/e2e verification is honored, not re-adjudicated |

## Requirement

_To be specified when promoted. Sketch: parse the legacy prose headers (`### REQ-NNN:`,
`- **Status:**`, `- **Depends on:**`, the acceptance bullets) into frontmatter + a body,
then reuse REQ-010's normalizer to inject `kind`, transcode acceptance, normalize
`supersedes`, and emit a synchronized index — reporting any header it cannot confidently
parse rather than guessing._

## Notes

Draft REQs produce no engine steps and are exempt from the acceptance-test lint rule.
Spawned by splitting REQ-010 during the memzy intake (2026-06-08), so the prose-converter
intent is not lost while REQ-010 narrows to memzy's frontmatter dialect.
