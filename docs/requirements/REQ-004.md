## Context

The shipped profile layers requirement knowledge onto the generic core: each active REQ
becomes a Design → Build → Land cycle, and whole requirements are sequenced by their
`depends_on` while independent ones interleave.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Three steps per active REQ; land carries the acceptance tests | Verification attaches where the work lands |
| 2 | `REQ-B:design` depends on `REQ-A:land` for each dependency | Sequence whole REQs without serializing everything |
| 3 | Done deps dropped; non-active/missing deps leave dependent blocked | Correct eligibility without side effects |

## Requirement

`ReqStepSource` derives steps from `docs/requirements/`. Terminal/draft REQs produce no
steps. The land step's `verify` is the REQ's acceptance `test:` commands.

```yaml acceptance
- id: AC1
  text: An active REQ yields design→build→land steps with the right intra-REQ dependencies.
  test: "python -m pytest tests/test_req_profile.py::test_three_steps_per_active_req"
```
