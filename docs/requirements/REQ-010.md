## Context

memzy and examengineer hold dozens of REQs in the older prose format (`### REQ-NNN:` with
`- **Status:**` lines). A converter could lift them into the hybrid machine-readable
format so existing projects can adopt the engine without a manual rewrite.

Explicitly **out of scope for the baseline** (plan 0001) — captured here as a draft so the
backlog is visible. Promote to `open` when a consumer actually needs it.

## Decisions
