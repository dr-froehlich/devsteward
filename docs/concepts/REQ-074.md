# REQ-074 concept — the `steward decision` surface, re-founded

**Decided: 2026-07-05, with Peter (attended develop session, concept phase per REQ-074
Decision 1).** Direction: **re-found** — neither the intake-leaned pure shrink nor the
pure reshape, but two moves: subtract the non-fork producers from the decisions
namespace, then build the guided decision loop for the genuine forks that remain.

## 1. The premise, answered

The REQ asked whether `steward decision` earns its surface at all. Peter's answer
(2026-07-05, this session): **yes — but for one job only.** The decision mechanism is
the autopilot raising the captain: when an *unattended* run hits an unforeseen fork
(a concept phase that should have happened but didn't, assumptions that broke, a
technical surprise), the engine must pause that strand rather than paper over the fork
on autopilot. **Rarity is not evidence against a safety valve** — engine fires are rare
too. The 2-of-34 / 0-of-8 numbers (below) do not indict the job; they show the
mechanism has almost never fired *for* its job, because its namespace was colonized by
things that are not forks.

Corollaries fixed by Peter in the same exchange:

- **An open validation is an open validation, not an open decision.** The validation
  tooling (`steward validate` / `rework` / `revalidate`) is sound; those holds must stop
  masquerading as decisions.
- The expected resolution UX mirrors the attended `manual`-AC validation: a **guided
  session** that briefs the operator on the fork, answers questions, gives a
  recommendation, records the human's choice, and knows how to continue the work.
  Flight sequence: `steward run` → "need your vote, can't stay on auto" → guided
  decision → `steward run`, fork cleared.

## 2. Producer inventory (post-REQ-056, code as of 2026-07-05)

Every site in the engine that creates a `Decision` record:

| # | Producer | Step | A genuine fork? | Today's resolution path |
|---|----------|------|-----------------|------------------------|
| 1 | `executor._park_attended` | `:develop` | **No** — a scheduling hold ("this step needs a human present", `concept: true` / split develop in batch) | `decision answer` is *not* guarded here; answering flips BLOCKED→PENDING, batch re-runs, re-parks — the circular trap REQ-056 removed for D/H |
| 2 | `executor._detect_park` (skill-written decision or `PARK_SENTINEL`) | `:develop` | **Yes — the only genuine-fork producer left** (park-and-surface §4) | `decision answer` free text; see the broken-channel finding below |
| 3 | `validate._park_manual` | `:validate` | No — awaiting the human oracle | REQ-057 guard refuses `answer`, redirects to `steward validate` |
| 4 | `validate._park_pending` | `:validate` | No — async QA standing item | same guard |
| 5 | `validate._park_red` | `:validate` | A two-branch fork, but both branches already have dedicated verbs | same guard, redirects to `rework` / `revalidate` |

`repair_exhausted` parks **no** decision since REQ-056 (mechanical go-fix-and-retry →
`steward repeat`).

**Empirical usage.** DevSteward's own ledger: 8 parks ever — 7 were `:validate` holds,
and the single `:develop` park (DEC-005) was a mechanical missing-plan retry, exactly
the class REQ-056 drained. Zero genuine design forks. FlowSteward (2026-07-02 process
review §1.2): 2 of 34 historical parks were genuine forks.

**The decisive code finding — the answer channel is write-only.** `Decision.answer` has
zero consumers: `answer_decision` records it in `state.yaml` and the event log, flips
the step BLOCKED→PENDING, and the resumed step re-runs its **unchanged**
`step.command`. No code path ever reads the answer back or delivers it to the resuming
session; the session can re-derive the same fork and re-park. `Decision.options` exists
in the model and has never been populated. So the shrink-cost the intake flagged ("the
free-text answer is the only channel handing the operator's guidance to the resuming
session") is a phantom — that channel does not actually work today. Likewise
`rework`/`revalidate` key on the red **validation event**, not on the decision record
(`_answer_validate_decision` tolerates "none parked") — the `:validate` decision
records are already dead weight.

## 3. The direction: re-found in two moves

### Move 1 — subtraction: restore the namespace

Producers 1, 3, 4, 5 stop creating `Decision` records. A blocked step is surfaced as a
**hold** by `steward status` (with its reason and its real verb: `steward validate`,
`rework`/`revalidate`, or "run this step attended"), not as a decision. The REQ-057
answer-guard for `:validate` decisions becomes unreachable for new parks (kept only as
legacy handling — see §5). After this move, `decision list` contains genuine forks
only — usually empty, which is correct for an alarm panel.

### Move 2 — the guided decision loop (mirrors the validate pattern)

For producer 2, the genuine skill-raised fork:

1. **Park with a brief.** A skill that parks must capture a fork brief — what was
   tried, the options as it sees them with consequences, its recommendation.
   `Decision.options` finally gets populated; richer context travels through the ledger
   (like rework's evidence), never on the command line. `decision list` renders it.
2. **`steward decide DEC-NNN`** — attended-only, same shape-A pattern as
   `steward validate`: refuses under CLAUDECODE (never spawn Claude from Claude),
   brings up a guided session that presents the brief, lets the operator interrogate
   the repo and ask for advice live; the **engine** records the operator's chosen
   option + rationale after the session exits (editor pattern — the session cannot
   self-certify), answers the decision, and unblocks the step.
3. **Delivery on resume.** The engine injects the answered fork (question, chosen
   option, rationale) into the resumed step's prompt — the same mechanism as
   `--repair` carrying the failure brief. This is the missing wire; without it the
   rest is theater.

## 4. Rejected alternatives

- **Pure shrink (the intake lean):** delete the answer path, redirect develop parks to
  attended re-runs. Rejected because it deletes the safety-valve *resolution* half
  outright — Peter wants the captain summoned *with a briefing and a recorder*, not
  told to fly the leg himself.
- **Pure reshape (options + per-option engine action on the existing surface):**
  rejected as machinery without the namespace cleanup; it would keep presenting holds
  as decisions and hard-code engine actions per option where a guided session is the
  better oracle.

## 5. Guardrails and boundaries

- **Junk parks are fixed at the producer.** The historical false parks (live-vs-
  regression mixups, `.env` blindness) were root-fixed by REQ-063/068/072. If a new
  class of junk parks appears, the fix is at that producer — never more decision UX.
- **Legacy records:** existing `:validate`/attended decision records in consumer
  ledgers (FlowSteward) must stay resolvable — the REQ-057 redirect and the REQ-073
  reconcile path are kept for records that predate this REQ.
- **Out of scope** (unchanged from intake): the lost-update guard and stale-decision
  recovery (REQ-073); the unattended park-and-surface *contract* beyond what the loop
  above requires.

## 6. Derived acceptance criteria

These replace the provisional intake block in `docs/requirements/REQ-074.md` (AC1 is
retained as the direction sign-off; AC2–AC6 are the direction-specific criteria this
concept derives; AC7 is the end-to-end human oracle).

- **AC2 (regression):** validation holds park no decision — `_park_manual`,
  `_park_pending`, `_park_red` leave no `Decision` record; the hold (reason + real
  verb) is surfaced by `steward status`; `rework`/`revalidate` still work on a red
  validation without any decision record.
- **AC3 (regression):** the attended-hold parks no decision — `_park_attended` records
  a hold, surfaced with its `attended_reason` and the "run attended" pointer; the
  circular answer trap is structurally gone.
- **AC4 (regression):** a genuine skill fork parks with a brief — the skill-written /
  `PARK_SENTINEL` path populates the question and options/recommendation;
  `decision list` renders the brief.
- **AC5 (regression):** `steward decide DEC-NNN` refuses inside a Claude session
  (CLAUDECODE) and under `DEVSTEWARD_UNATTENDED=1`; on a recorded choice it stores the
  chosen option + rationale, appends `decision_answered`, and flips the step
  BLOCKED→PENDING (never resurrecting a DONE step — REQ-073 replay rule).
- **AC6 (regression):** delivery on resume — the resumed step's command carries the
  answered fork (question, chosen option, rationale), analogous to the `--repair`
  failure brief.
- **AC7 (manual):** end-to-end walk-through — Peter drives `steward decide` on a
  staged fork in a plain session (guided brief → interrogation → recorded choice →
  `steward run` resumes with the fork delivered) and signs off the UX.
